Delete your Omniflick data

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Omniflick doesn't need an account. If you signed in with Google to sync, the app created a sync account linked to your Google account. This page explains how to delete that account and all your data, with or without the app installed.

Delete your sync account and cloud data

  1. Email the developer at the address shown under "Developer contact" on Omniflick's Google Play listing, from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Omniflick data". If you can't email from that account, tell us the email address of the account, and we may ask you to confirm it.
  2. We delete your synced data and your sign-in record within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What's deleted: everything synced to your account (profiles, ratings, My List, watched history, "Not interested" list, settings and any synced TMDB / OMDb keys) and your Firebase sign-in record (email address, account ID and sign-in dates).

What's kept: nothing linked to you. Anonymous crash reports, which aren't linked to your account, are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Delete data on a device

  • Everything on one device: Android Settings → Apps → Omniflick → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. This also removes Ask AI keys and the Recent log.
  • Stop syncing on one device: in Omniflick, Settings → Sign out on this device. Your data stays in your account until you ask for it to be deleted.
  • Ask AI key only: Settings → Ask AI → remove the key.
  • Recent log: Settings → Recent log → clear it.
  • Crash reports: Settings → Recent log → turn Crash reports off. This also deletes reports not yet sent. Reports already sent are deleted after 90 days, or sooner if you ask.

Remove Omniflick's access to your Google account

Go to myaccount.google.com/connections, choose Omniflick (or StreamHub TV, its earlier name) and remove its access. If you used an early version that synced through Google Drive, this also lets you delete the hidden app file it saved there.
